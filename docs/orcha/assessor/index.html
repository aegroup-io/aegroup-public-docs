Evidence discipline — claims someone else can audit

Every fact has a provenance mode, an exact locator, and a stable ID. Interpretation lives in claims; contradictions are preserved until explicitly resolved.

Three collection modes

static-artifactFiles read in place — never imported or executed
supplied-renderScreenshots and recordings the owner provides
owner-inputRecorded answers from the people who know

Modes identify provenance, never permission — none authorizes executing the target or calling anything it names.

A closed locator grammar

Every evidence row points at an exact place, in a grammar validators can check:

file:src/app.py#L41-L60
render:checkout.png#region=cart
record:owner-qa.md#L12

Cited lines are verified to exist by counting bytes — never by importing code or opening a URL.

Claims, unknowns, blockers

A specialist claim is observed or inferred, cites supporting and contradicting evidence, and names what it affects. What can't be supported becomes an UNK-* unknown with the evidence needed to resolve it — never a guess with a hedge.

claims: observed | inferred · unknowns: UNK-* · blockers: BLOCK-*

Confidence is derived, not asserted

An inferred claim caps its subject at medium; a lower-confidence claim caps at its own level; a retained contradiction forces low plus a critical evidence gap — and the whole decision becomes needs-evidence until it's resolved and recorded.

One disposition registry

Every surfaced capability, baseline requirement, decision, and risk lands in accepted-references.json as accepted, deferred, or rejected — each with nonempty evidence support and a substantive rationale. Markdown views render from it; prose cannot override it.

Stable IDs everywhere

Cross-references resolve exactly or the package is invalid — no orphans, no duplicates, no cross-wiring.

The decision — scored, gated, kept separate

Hard eligibility gates run before any weighted preference; evidence caps confidence; and no strategy makes the production gate pass automatically.

12 criteria · 0–4 scale

architecture alignment, reproducibility, behavioral legibility, platform replacement ratio, replacement risk, interface boundary quality… — each score bound to a typed criterion claim in the evidence ledger

→

Deterministic scorer

score_adoption.py emits option scores, eligibility, blockers, the winning strategy, evidence-capped confidence, and the rubric version — same result for the same input, every time

Adopt

Keep a meaningful portion of the implementation and adapt it. Right when the architecture already fits, the build reproduces, and platform-owned replacement is low.

gate: build must be reproducible

Reconstruct

Treat the source as evidence and rebuild accepted behavior on the platform baseline. Right when behavior is recoverable but most of the shell would be replaced.

the common verdict for vibe-coded apps

Wrap

Preserve the runtime temporarily behind an explicit boundary and replace it incrementally. Requires an isolatable interface and a written exit plan — never permanence by accident.

boundary quality ≥ 2 + retirement condition

needs-evidence

When a mandatory fact is unknown, no strategy is forced. The assessment asks for safe artifacts or owner answers instead of relaxing its constraints.

critical gap ⇒ the whole decision waits

The integrator re-checks contracts, references, scorer output, reconstruction, and publication readiness with 21 dependency-free scripts. Evidence roots must stay strictly disjoint from the output root — a report can never become its own evidence.

Publication handoff

ready_to_publish means the validated blocker state is clear — it is technical readiness, never permission. Denied authorization blocks; a non-ready production gate blocks; explicit approval is recorded separately and clears nothing else.

preview (offline) → preflight (read-only) → apply (approved)
